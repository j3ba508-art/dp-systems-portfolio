What it is

Pause for one hour before you spend on software.

Thinking about buying software to fix a messy process? This workbook helps you identify one costly process problem, find what is really causing it, and decide what to fix next, before you spend money on a tool that may not solve it.

What you will do

Work through seven short steps with one real process from your business:

  1. Choose one problem
  2. Map what happens now
  3. Find and price the costliest friction
  4. Improve the process first
  5. Choose the simplest adequate fix
  6. Explain what the solution must do
  7. Write your one-page Workflow Action Brief

What you walk away with

A clear one-page Workflow Action Brief that points to your next move: simplify the process, improve a tool you already have, or talk to a software provider with a brief they can build from.

What is inside

Part 1, the 60-minute session: Steps 1 to 7, fill-in pages and a worked example of a multi-branch clinic.

Part 2, After the Hour: vendor questions, a two-week test, access roles, exceptions, rollout and written procedures.

Who it is for

Small business owners and operations managers with one process that causes repeated delays, errors, complaints or unnecessary admin. It is not a course in software or systems analysis, and it does not recommend a specific product.
