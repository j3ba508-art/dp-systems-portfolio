Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal information I collect when you visit my site, buy a product or contact me, and how I use it. I am Jameson Yao, doing business as D & P Systems, based in Lucena, Calabarzon, Philippines. I handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

What I collect

  • When you buy: my payment platform, currently Lemon Squeezy, collects your name, email address, billing country or address and payment details to process the order. I do not see or store your card number. I receive order details through my seller dashboard, such as your name, email address, country, order number and what you bought.
  • When you contact me: the name, email address and message you send. The contact form on this site opens your email app, so I only receive what you send me by email.
  • When you sign up for the free page: the email address you type into the sign-up form, and the fact that you agreed to receive the page and occasional emails about the workbook. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email, or by writing to me.
  • When you send feedback: the answers you type into the Reader Feedback form. The form does not ask for your email address. You choose whether to give a name for credit.
  • When you visit the site: the site’s hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view.

How I use it

  • to deliver your purchase and answer your questions;
  • to send you the free page and, if you agreed, occasional emails about the workbook;
  • to help with problems and, where needed, fix an order;
  • to improve my products using your feedback;
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

I do not sell your personal information. I only send you emails if you asked me to, for example by signing up for the free page, or if I am replying to you.

Who else handles your information

I use a few services to run the store, and they process information for their own purposes as well as mine:

  • Lemon Squeezy, and any other platform I sell through (payment, tax handling and receipts, as merchant of record);
  • Supabase (database where sign-up email addresses are stored);
  • MailerLite (email service used to send emails to people who signed up);
  • Google (the Reader Feedback form and its response spreadsheet);
  • Vercel (hosting for this website).

These services may store information on servers outside the Philippines. Each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies

This site does not ask you to create an account. Checkout pages run by my payment platform set their own cookies, which are described in that platform’s policy.

How long I keep it

I keep order records for as long as the law and my accounting duties require. I keep emails and feedback only as long as I need them for the purpose they were given. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, to stop using it for a purpose, or to withdraw consent you gave. Contact me at the address below. For payment data held by my payment platform, you can also contact them directly.

Children

My products are for business owners and managers, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

I limit access to the information I hold and rely on the security of the services above. No online service is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
